Singapore Telco Forum Sentiment Dashboard

Scope: All telcos · Sources: Reddit (r/askSingapore, r/singapore) + HWZ · Sample: 192 telco-relevant posts · Generated from labeled_sample.csv + hwz_telco_posts_labeled.csv

Executive Summary

Forum discussion is moderately positive on balance (~60% happy), but tone varies sharply by platform: Reddit is more complaint-driven (32% negative) while HWZ skews toward launch chatter (14% negative). Pricing and MVNO value dominate conversation, especially around Eight's launch and legacy MNO plan costs. Network quality is location- and brand-tier-dependent; customer-service friction (porting, billing, promo fulfilment) drives the strongest negative spikes for StarHub and Singtel. Among brands with enough mentions, Giga and Eight attract the most scepticism, while Singtel and Zym appear relatively less negative.

Recommendations

  • Disclose host-network and MVNO priority tiering transparently at launch.
  • Fix porting and identity-document edge cases (passport validity, dropdown gaps).
  • Market bundled roaming clearly — Malaysia threads are a key decision moment.
  • Avoid "low price hurts quality" messaging without evidence; it triggers switching intent.
  • Use location-based network proof points over national coverage claims.

Key Issue Insights

  • Pricing: MVNO disruption vs legacy $72/3GB MNO plans; CEO rhetoric backlash.
  • Network: Hyper-local coverage; M1 dead zones; MVNO deprioritisation fears.
  • Customer service: StarHub porting blocks, Comex promo delays, Singtel ONR lock-in.
  • Porting: Eight no port-in at launch; Eight missing from StarHub dropdown.
  • Roaming: MVNOs (Vivifi, Giga, GOMO) mostly positive in Malaysia threads.

Limitations

Sample size 192 telco-relevant rows — not nationally representative. Reddit over-indexes on complaints; HWZ over-indexes on Eight launch threads. No issue_primary in Reddit export (themes inferred). Sentiment auto-labels show sarcasm/context errors. Cannot infer churn, NPS, or network KPIs — only stated forum perceptions.
