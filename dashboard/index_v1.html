Executive Summary

An early-warning screen, not CSAT or NPS: 1,711 unique posts in 105 telco threads (2019-03-26 to 2026-04-09; 74% from 2026). The three largest threads hold 20% of posts; the biggest is “16 March 26: Singtel mobile service outage”. GPT-4o-mini, the labeller that best matched human labels (macro F1 0.631 vs VADER 0.360), reads 55% of posts as not happy and 17% as happy; VADER on the same posts says 32% and 40%. Network Quality leads (45.3% with every thread weighted equally, 52.8% per post), then Mobile Plans (13.4%). Network Quality carries the most not-happy discussion. Using the same labeller on both sources, Reddit is 60% not happy and HWZ 40% (the platforms also discuss different topics). Among brands with at least 17 posts and 10 threads, Singtel (63% not happy, n=1139) and StarHub (48% not happy, n=361) show the highest not-happy share. In the weekly huddle view, 3 week(s) crossed the huddle threshold (n ≥ 20), the largest 352 posts in the week of 2026-03-16. Sentiment by GPT-4o-mini; issues by keyword rules plus GPT-4o.

How to use this dashboard

Weekly 30-minute huddle. Charts triage; people decide.

A. Queue

Not-happy posts in Network Quality, App & Account, or Porting that week. Act only if n ≥ 20.

B. Open 10

Read ten source posts. Drop sarcasm, mixed-brand, and off-target labels.

C. Match

Tie remaining posts to tickets, outages, or billing — not to a vibe.

D. One owner

Assign a squad and a response date. No public brand ranking.

E. Next week

Check whether that issue share fell. If not, escalate the same owner.

What operators should do, in order of not-happy share

  • Network Network Quality: 52.5% of not-happy, 45.3% of all (thread-weighted)
    Join named-location and outage posts to tickets and cell data; publish status and ETA during incidents. Check: Tickets fall at that location; fewer “is it down?” threads.
  • Product Mobile Plans: 12.1% of not-happy, 13.4% of all (thread-weighted)
    Show fair-use limits, add-on charges (e.g. Caller ID), recurring price and host network at checkout. Check: Fewer “which plan / which backend?” threads.
  • Service operations Customer Service: 4.9% of not-happy, 4.1% of all (thread-weighted)
    Keep a self-service path and callback queue open during incidents; close escalations with a named owner. Check: Fewer “cannot reach support” posts after the next incident.
  • Commercial Pricing & Promotions: 4.8% of not-happy, 6.0% of all (thread-weighted)
    Publish intro vs recurring price and promo expiry; audit promotion fulfilment. Check: Fewer fulfilment complaints, not just a lower list price.
  • Device support Devices: 4.7% of not-happy, 6.0% of all (thread-weighted)
    Document router, ONR/ONT and eSIM requirements per plan. Check: Fewer equipment and bridge-mode threads.
  • Billing Billing & Payment: 4.5% of not-happy, 4.7% of all (thread-weighted)
    Publish the outage compensation rule with the incident notice; explain charges before the bill lands. Check: Fewer compensation and surprise-charge posts.

How to read the themes

  • Network Quality: Coverage, dead zones, outages and speed.
  • Pricing & Promotions: Price-value comparisons, discounts and promotion fulfilment.
  • Mobile Plans: SIM-only allowances, contract terms and plan comparisons.
  • Roaming: Regional bundles, activation and overseas use.
  • Porting: Port-in and port-out, rejects and switching.
  • Customer Service: Hotline, agent and escalation experiences.
  • Billing & Payment: Unexpected charges, refunds and payment handling.
  • App & Account: Login, verification and self-service app friction.
  • Devices: Phones, eSIM/SIM cards, routers and operator equipment.
  • General Discussion: Telco news and opinions without a specific service issue.

Limitations

1,711 unique posts from self-selecting Reddit and HWZ users, not Singapore subscribers. 74% are from 2026 and the largest thread is “16 March 26: Singtel mobile service outage”, so brand and network shares describe those events as much as the market. Posts in a thread are not independent; compare per-post and thread-weighted shares. Sentiment is GPT-4o-mini, which scored macro F1 0.631 against human labels (VADER 0.360). One sentiment is copied to every brand a post names. Issue tags are keyword rules plus GPT-4o on unresolved rows; the corpus reassignments have no human check. This is a triage queue, not NPS, churn or market share. This is the original corpus (posts to 9 Apr 2026), kept as a case study of the March 2026 Singtel outage; the main findings use the larger v2 corpus (index_v2.html).
