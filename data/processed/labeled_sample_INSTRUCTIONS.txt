Manual labeling instructions
========================================

1. Open the CSV in Excel or Google Sheets.
2. For each row, fill in:
   - sentiment_label_manual: one of  happy | not_happy | neutral
   - issue_manual: Network Quality, Pricing & Promotions, Mobile Plans, Roaming,
     Porting, Customer Service, Billing & Payment, App & Account, Devices, or
     General Discussion (comma-separated when needed)
   - notes: optional
3. Save as labeled_sample.csv (or another name) for use in evaluation.
Keep annotators blind to all model outputs for formal evaluation.
