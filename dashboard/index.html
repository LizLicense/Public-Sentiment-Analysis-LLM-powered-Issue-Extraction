Executive Summary

An early-warning screen, not CSAT or NPS: 9,162 unique posts in 349 telco threads (2019-03-26 to 2026-09-26; 95% from 2026). The three largest threads hold 10% of posts; the biggest is “zym.sg Mobile (Singtel MVNO)”. GPT-4o-mini, the labeller that best matched human labels (macro F1 0.641 vs VADER 0.380), reads 44% of posts as not happy and 19% as happy; VADER on the same posts says 28% and 46%. Network Quality leads (36.9% with every thread weighted equally, 40.7% per post), then Mobile Plans (12.6%). Network Quality carries the most not-happy discussion. Using the same labeller on both sources, Reddit is 48% not happy and HWZ 37% (the platforms also discuss different topics). Among brands with at least 92 posts and 10 threads, Singtel (48% not happy, n=3970) and Giga (46% not happy, n=360) show the highest not-happy share. In the weekly huddle view, 2 week(s) crossed the huddle threshold (n ≥ 115 (twice the median week of 57.5 over the last 26 weeks)), the largest 352 posts in the week of 2026-03-16. Sentiment by GPT-4o-mini; issues by keyword rules plus GPT-4o.

How to use this dashboard

Weekly 30-minute huddle. Charts triage; people decide.

A. Queue

Not-happy posts in Network Quality, App & Account, or Porting that week. Act only if n ≥ 115 (twice the median week of 57.5 over the last 26 weeks).

B. Open 10

Read ten source posts. Drop sarcasm, mixed-brand, and off-target labels.

C. Match

Tie remaining posts to tickets, outages, or billing — not to a vibe.

D. One owner

Assign a squad and a response date. No public brand ranking.

E. Next week

Check whether that issue share fell. If not, escalate the same owner.

What operators should do, in order of not-happy share

  • Network Network Quality: 46.2% of not-happy, 36.9% of all (thread-weighted)
    Join named-location and outage posts to tickets and cell data; publish status and ETA during incidents. Check: Tickets fall at that location; fewer “is it down?” threads.
  • Product Mobile Plans: 13.0% of not-happy, 12.6% of all (thread-weighted)
    Show fair-use limits, add-on charges (e.g. Caller ID), recurring price and host network at checkout. Check: Fewer “which plan / which backend?” threads.
  • Commercial Pricing & Promotions: 6.7% of not-happy, 6.6% of all (thread-weighted)
    Publish intro vs recurring price and promo expiry; audit promotion fulfilment. Check: Fewer fulfilment complaints, not just a lower list price.
  • Device support Devices: 5.7% of not-happy, 6.5% of all (thread-weighted)
    Document router, ONR/ONT and eSIM requirements per plan. Check: Fewer equipment and bridge-mode threads.
  • Service operations Customer Service: 4.6% of not-happy, 3.9% of all (thread-weighted)
    Keep a self-service path and callback queue open during incidents; close escalations with a named owner. Check: Fewer “cannot reach support” posts after the next incident.
  • Roaming Roaming: 4.5% of not-happy, 4.9% of all (thread-weighted)
    In-app “travelling to X” checklist: activation, coverage, overage cap. Check: Fewer destination bill-shock posts.

How to read the themes

  • Network Quality: Coverage, dead zones, outages and speed.
  • Pricing & Promotions: Price-value comparisons, discounts and promotion fulfilment.
  • Mobile Plans: SIM-only allowances, contract terms and plan comparisons.
  • Roaming: Regional bundles, activation and overseas use.
  • Porting: Port-in and port-out, rejects and switching.
  • Customer Service: Hotline, agent and escalation experiences.
  • Billing & Payment: Unexpected charges, refunds and payment handling.
  • App & Account: Login, verification and self-service app friction.
  • Devices: Phones, eSIM/SIM cards, routers and operator equipment.
  • General Discussion: Telco news and opinions without a specific service issue.

Limitations

9,162 unique posts from self-selecting Reddit and HWZ users, not Singapore subscribers. 95% are from 2026 and the largest thread is “zym.sg Mobile (Singtel MVNO)”, so brand and network shares describe those events as much as the market. Posts in a thread are not independent; compare per-post and thread-weighted shares. Sentiment is GPT-4o-mini, which scored macro F1 0.641 against human labels (VADER 0.380). One sentiment is copied to every brand a post names. Issue tags are keyword rules plus GPT-4o on unresolved rows; the corpus reassignments have no human check. This is a triage queue, not NPS, churn or market share. 1 post(s) had no GPT-4o-mini label and use VADER. Posts from 10 Apr to 26 Sep 2026 come from the newest HWZ thread pages and, for Reddit, the Arctic Shift research archive (posts and replies). On a separate 97-post gold set of those recent posts, GPT-4o-mini scored macro F1 0.637 (VADER 0.428) but read many complaints as neutral, so not-happy shares here are likely understated.
