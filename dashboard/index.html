Executive Summary

This dashboard is an early-warning screen, not a CSAT or NPS score. About 61% of 158 telco-relevant posts look happy, but VADER under-detects complaints, so the true negative share is likely higher. Start complaint review on Reddit (30% not-happy vs 15% on HWZ); HWZ is launch-thread heavy, not happier customers. Route App & Account (25%) and Network Quality (26%) first. Among brands with at least 10 mentions, Eight and StarHub warrant deeper review. Do not rank Giga, SIMBA or Zym from tiny samples. The operating use is a weekly 30-minute huddle: this sample has 13 not-happy Network/App/Porting posts (act only if n ≥ 20). Never publish a brand league table from n=6.

How to use this dashboard

Weekly 30-minute huddle. Charts triage; people decide. This is the operating purpose of the project.

A. Queue

B. Open 10

Read ten source posts. Drop sarcasm, mixed-brand, and off-target labels.

C. Match

Tie remaining posts to tickets, outages, or billing — not to a vibe.

D. One owner

Assign a squad and a response date. No public brand ranking.

E. Next week

Check whether that issue share fell. If not, escalate the same owner.

What operators should do

  • CX / digital: Own App & Account (login, OTP, chatbot dead-ends). Track time-to-human and login-failure posts after each app release.
  • Network: Pair MRT/building complaints with tickets. Publish indoor/MRT guidance instead of national coverage claims.
  • MVNO / product: Disclose host network, 5G SA and fair-use at checkout (GOMO/Singtel, Eight/StarHub, Zym).
  • MNP ops: Show a specific port-reject reason and next step. Own avoidable-reject rate and median port time.
  • Commercial: Publish intro vs recurring price and promo expiry. Monitor fulfilment complaints, not list price alone.
  • Roaming: In-app “travelling to X” checklist (activation, 3G sunset, divert charges) before the bill.
  • Insight huddle: Follow the five-step playbook at the top of this page. Do not skip to brand ranks.

How to read the themes

  • Network Quality: Hyper-local coverage, dead zones, outages and MVNO priority concerns.
  • Pricing & Promotions: Price-value comparisons, discounts and promotion fulfilment.
  • Mobile Plans: SIM-only allowances, contract terms and plan-feature comparisons.
  • Roaming: Regional bundles, activation and Malaysia travel experiences.
  • Porting: Eight port-in limits; brand missing from port dropdowns.
  • Customer Service: Hotline, agent and escalation experiences.
  • Billing & Payment: Unexpected charges, refunds and payment handling.
  • App & Account: Login, verification and self-service app friction.
  • Devices: Phones, eSIM/SIM cards, routers and operator equipment.
  • General Discussion: Telco news, opinions and records without a specific service issue.

Limitations

n=158 telco-relevant posts from Reddit + HWZ — not Singapore subscribers. The 15,504-row corpus is ~97% Reddit, with heavy duplicate text and ~150 unique threads. Dashboard mix (67 Reddit / 91 HWZ) is not the corpus mix. Sentiment is VADER and likely too positive; independent labels show not-happy recall of 0.34. Issue tags are keyword rules plus GPT-4o on unresolved rows; all-row GPT-4o still misses Devices, which is why rules are kept first. Brands with n<10 are shown in the table only. This is a triage queue, not NPS, churn or market share. Issue labels: keyword rules then GPT-4o on unresolved general_discussion (97 of 158 posts matched to data/processed/issues_posts_labeled_2026-08-21_203752_gpt4o_hybrid.csv).
