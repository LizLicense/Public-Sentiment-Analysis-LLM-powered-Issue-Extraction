Executive Summary

Forum discussion is moderately positive on balance (~60% happy), but tone varies sharply by platform: Reddit is more complaint-driven (32% negative) while HWZ skews toward launch/plan threads (14% negative). Pricing and MVNO value dominate conversation (23% of posts). Network quality is location- and brand-tier-dependent; customer-service friction drives negative spikes for StarHub and Singtel. Among brands with enough mentions, Giga and Eight attract the most scepticism, while SIMBA appears relatively less negative.

Recommendations

  • Disclose host-network and MVNO priority tiering transparently at launch.
  • Fix porting and identity-document edge cases (passport validity, dropdown gaps).
  • Market bundled roaming clearly — Malaysia threads are a key decision moment.
  • Avoid "low price hurts quality" messaging without evidence; it triggers switching intent.
  • Use location-based network proof points over national coverage claims.

Key Issue Insights

  • Pricing: MVNO disruption vs legacy MNO plans; CEO rhetoric backlash.
  • Network: Hyper-local coverage; M1 dead zones; MVNO deprioritisation fears.
  • Customer service: StarHub porting blocks, promo delays, Singtel ONR lock-in.
  • Porting: Eight port-in limits; brand missing from port dropdowns.
  • Roaming: MVNOs (Vivifi, Giga, GOMO) mostly positive in Malaysia threads.

Limitations

Sample size 193 telco-relevant rows — not nationally representative. Reddit over-indexes on complaints; HWZ over-indexes on launch threads. Issue themes inferred from keywords. Sentiment auto-labels may misread sarcasm/thread context. Cannot infer churn, NPS, or network KPIs.
